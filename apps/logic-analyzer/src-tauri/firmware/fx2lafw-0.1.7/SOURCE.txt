These .fw files are the unmodified images of sigrok's binary release
  https://sigrok.org/download/binary/sigrok-firmware-fx2lafw/sigrok-firmware-fx2lafw-bin-0.1.7.tar.gz
  (SHA-256 c876fd075549e7783a6d5bfc8d99a695cfc583ddbcea0217d8e3f9351d1723af)
They are a separate program that runs on the logic analyzer's FX2 chip. Voltrus Logic Analyzer only
uploads them to the board over USB; they are not part of the app's code.
